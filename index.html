                      About Under the City

                      Under the City helps New York students find free or low-cost places to study, practice, and get help, matched to their budget, schedule, and how far they can travel.

                      How listings are checked

                      Every listing comes from a directory we built by hand. We only add a place after opening its official page, and we copy hours, cost, and ages exactly as written there.

                      When a source doesn't say something, the app shows "Unknown. Check the source." instead of guessing. Each listing shows the date we last checked it and links to where the information came from.

                      How results are picked

                      The app removes anything that's the wrong day or time, costs too much, doesn't fit your age, or is too far. It ranks the rest by how well they match and how close they are. Distances are straight-line from the center of the neighborhood you pick, so they're shown as "about" a number of miles, not travel time.

                      Nothing books or registers you. Each listing tells you how to sign up on the program's own site.

                      Your privacy

                      The app asks for a neighborhood, never your exact location. Your age, neighborhood, interests, and saved listings stay in this browser. There are no accounts.

                      A shared list or invite link contains only which listings are on it, nothing about you.

                      Something wrong?

                      Hours change. If a listing is out of date, use "Report a problem" on that listing, or email us.
